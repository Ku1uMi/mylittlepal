# Addressing a Human Need: Helping International Students De-stress

## Problem Statement
Moving to a new country for college is incredibly tough. Many international students deal with intense schoolwork while experiencing deep loneliness because they are far away from their families and childhood friends. This isolation can lead to a lot of stress, anxiety, and homesickness. 

Research shows that spending time with a pet is a great way to lower stress and feel less lonely. However, most international students cannot have a real pet because:
* University dorms and apartments usually ban animals.
* Vet bills and pet food are too expensive on a student budget.
* Students are too busy with classes to properly care for a living animal.
* It is extremely complicated and expensive to move a pet back home across international borders after graduation.

## Our Solution: "Your Little Pal"
"Your Little Pal" solves this problem by giving students a cute, pixel-art digital pet that they can keep right on their phones for quick, relaxing mental breaks.

### How it's different from other virtual pet apps:
Most virtual pet games (like old-school Tamagotchis) can actually make you *more* stressed out because the pet will permanently die if you forget to check on it, or the app constantly pesters you with annoying notifications. 

Our app is completely stress-free and non-punitive. Your pal won't die, and caring for it is designed to be a quick, calming activity to help you unwind between demanding classes.

### Using phone hardware to make it feel real:
Instead of just tapping a glass screen, we use the phone's built-in systems to make interacting with your pal feel more physical:
* **Interactive Gestures:** To wash your pet, you use real scrubbing motions (`onPanUpdate`) to clear away soap bubbles, which makes the care loop feel tactile and satisfying.
* **Real-World Context (Time & Environment):** The pet is hooked up to the phone's internal sensors (like the clock and ambient parameters). This means your pal reacts dynamically to your actual environment—like getting ready for bed when it gets dark outside—encouraging you to take healthy real-world breaks.

## Target Audience
The main audience is international university students who are dealing with stress and isolation, and who need a quick, free, and easy way to relax either at home or on campus.
