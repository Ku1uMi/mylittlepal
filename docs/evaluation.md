# Usability Evaluation

## 1. Methodology
We conducted a **System Usability Scale (SUS)** test with 4 participants to evaluate the usability of *Your Little Pal*. Each participant was asked to perform three core tasks before completing the 10-item SUS questionnaire:

1. Feeding the pet.
2. Purchasing an item in the shop.
3. Customizing the pet's outfit.

Participants were selected via convenience sampling to include a range of ages and technical backgrounds, providing a diverse perspective on the application's usability.

## 3. SUS Scoring Calculation
* Overall SUS Score:** 77.5 / 100
* Usability Subscore (Average of Q2, Q3, Q8): 3.5 / 5
* Learnability Subscore (Average of Q4, Q7, Q10): 3.8 / 5

## 4. Qualitative Feedback
In addition to the survey, we conducted brief follow-up interviews to gather qualitative data:
* Most frustrating part of the app: Users noted that the navigation between the shop and the pet screen could be smoother.
* Top requested feature: Users suggested adding a "favorites" tab for outfits to speed up customization.
