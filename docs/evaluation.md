You should evaluate and report on the usability of your application. You must SUS Test your app with at least 4 people. Raw data from all your participants should be included in this doc, along with the overall SUS score and the usability and learnability subscores.  You may also use additional methods to evaluate your app, including interviews and surveys (but if you survey, we want to know how you constructed an appropriate sample).
